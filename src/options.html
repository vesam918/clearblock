      Private by design

      ClearBlock does not collect browsing history, transmit analytics, use remote code, or require an account. Filtering runs through Chrome's Manifest V3 Declarative Net Request API.

      The YouTube profile is intentionally compatibility-first: it can relax ClearBlock on YouTube, but it does not impersonate Premium, remove enforcement messages, download videos, or manipulate YouTube’s player internals.

      ClearBlock is open source under GPL-3.0.
