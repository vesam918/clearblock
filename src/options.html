      Private by design

      ClearBlock does not collect browsing history, transmit analytics, use remote code, or require an account. Filtering runs through Chrome's Manifest V3 Declarative Net Request API.

      On YouTube, ClearBlock combines network filtering with removal of clearly identified rendered ad containers. It does not impersonate Premium, download videos, bypass access controls, or modify YouTube’s private player data.

      ClearBlock is open source under GPL-3.0.
